AVIF is a newer image format built on the AV1 video codec. At the same visual quality it often makes smaller files than WebP or JPG, and it supports transparency, wide color and HDR. Current versions of the major browsers display it, but support outside the browser is thin: many photo apps, editors, operating system previews and websites still refuse it. That gap, not the image itself, is the usual reason to turn an AVIF into an older format.
