MP4 is the video file that plays almost everywhere: phones, browsers, TVs, editing apps and social platforms accept it. Strictly speaking it is a container, a box that holds a video track, an audio track and sometimes subtitles. The video inside is most often H.264, which is why an MP4 so rarely refuses to play. Choose MP4 when a video has to be sent, posted or played on a device you don't control.
