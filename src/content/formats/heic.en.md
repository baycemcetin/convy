HEIC is the photo format iPhones and recent iPads save by default, and many Android phones offer it too. It stores a picture in noticeably less space than JPG at similar quality, which is why Apple chose it. The catch is compatibility: older Windows versions, many web forms, print shops and some editing programs cannot open it, and sharing one often ends with a request to send it again as JPG. HEIF is the same family of files and is treated as the same format here.
