HEIC is the format iPhones and recent iPads save photos in by default, and some Android phones offer it too. It usually stores a picture in less space than a JPG of similar quality, which is why Apple chose it. The catch is compatibility: older Windows versions, many web forms, print shops and some editing programs can't open it, and sharing one often ends with a request to send it again as a JPG. HEIF is the same family of files and is treated as the same format here.
