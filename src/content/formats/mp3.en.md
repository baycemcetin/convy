MP3 is the audio format every music player, phone, car stereo and browser understands. It is lossy: it shrinks sound by discarding what the ear is least likely to miss, so a song takes a fraction of the space it would as a WAV. Each new MP3 save loses a little more, so keep the original if you will edit the sound again. Choose MP3 when audio has to play anywhere, or when a recording needs to be small.
