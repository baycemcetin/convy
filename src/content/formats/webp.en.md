WebP is the image format Google made for the web, and often the reason a picture saved from a website turns out not to be a JPG. It can be lossy or lossless, keeps transparency and can even be animated, usually in smaller files than the JPG or PNG equivalent. Browsers open it without trouble; older photo editors, some Windows tools, print services and many web forms still don't. It suits images that live on a web page.
