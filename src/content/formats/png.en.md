PNG is the format for screenshots, logos, diagrams and anything with sharp edges or text. It is lossless: saving it again never changes a pixel. The price is size, since the same photo as a PNG is often several times larger than as a JPG. It supports transparency, so a logo can sit on any background, and every browser and operating system opens it. Choose it for graphics, for images you will keep editing, or whenever transparency matters.
