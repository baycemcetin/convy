M4A is audio in an MP4 container, usually encoded as AAC and sometimes as Apple Lossless (ALAC). It is what iTunes Store purchases and iPhone voice memos come as. AAC is generally considered to sound better than MP3 at the same bit rate, yet some older players, car stereos, web forms and editing tools still expect an MP3. That mismatch, rather than quality, is the usual reason to convert an M4A.
