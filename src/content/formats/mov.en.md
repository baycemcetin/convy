MOV is Apple's QuickTime video format, and what an iPhone camera or a Mac screen recording usually produces. Like MP4 it is a container. Recent iPhones often fill it with HEVC (H.265) video, which saves space but is still not played by many Windows PCs, browsers and websites. When a clip from an iPhone won't open somewhere else, the usual fix is turning the MOV into an MP4 with H.264 inside.
