WebM is an open video format made for the web, with VP8, VP9 or AV1 video and Opus or Vorbis audio inside. Screen recorders, browser-based tools and many video downloads produce it, and every major browser plays it. Outside the browser it is less welcome: iPhone apps, video editors, TVs and messaging apps often expect an MP4. Choose WebM for video that will only ever play on a web page.
