PDF is the format for documents that must look the same everywhere: invoices, contracts, forms, manuals, scanned pages. It fixes the layout, fonts and images in place, so the page you see is the page the other person sees, on any device. The same fixity makes a PDF awkward to edit or reuse, and a scanned PDF holds pictures of pages rather than selectable text. When a form or an office asks for a PDF, an image can become one.
